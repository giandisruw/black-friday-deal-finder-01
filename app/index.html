How we check

Every deal on this page was found and checked on Black Friday, 27 Nov 2026, by reading the price at the named store or in a deals source that names the store and the price. Each card says where its price was read and when. Discounts are computed from the two prices shown, so they always add up. A deal we could not verify is not listed.

  • Genuine low — the lowest price tracked for that product.
  • Strong price — a deep cut, well below the usual street price.
  • Solid cut — a good, honest discount, not a record low.
  • Modest cut — a small but real saving.
  • Read the fine print — a real saving with a condition, such as a coupon to clip or a “comparable value” reference price.

Two honest notes for this year: no PlayStation 5 or Xbox console is discounted for Black Friday 2026 — only the Nintendo Switch 2 bundle is cut — and Best Buy shows a “comparable value” next to some sale prices instead of a sale history, so on those deals the sale price itself is what counts.
